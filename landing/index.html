Frequently Asked Questions

❓Do I need to be fit to take this course?
No. This is not a workout program. You won’t find reps, calorie targets, or “no pain, no gain” talk here. This course is about using movement to shift your emotional state, regulate your thoughts, and build resilience — no matter where you’re starting from.
❓Is this course for me if I already work out?
Yes — especially if you’re working out but still feeling stuck.

This course isn’t about checking the “I exercised” box. It’s about learning to choose movement that matches your emotional state, builds the skills you want in life, and helps you feel more like you.

If your workouts feel disconnected, repetitive, or like they’re missing something deeper, this will help you reconnect. The goal isn’t just to move — it’s to move in a way that means something to you.
❓What if I hate exercise?
Good news: this isn’t about punishing your body. It’s about rebuilding your relationship with movement. The goal isn’t to sweat — it’s to listen. You’ll learn how to pick movement that feels right for you (and why it matters more than doing what’s trendy).

About your tutor

Mariele Klering is a Brazilian writer and resilience advocate based in New Zealand. After over a decade in the tech industry, she stepped onto the Jiu-Jitsu mat during a time of personal upheaval—and never left. Her writing explores the intersections of strength, loss, identity, and the quiet power of doing hard things. She is the author of The Mat as a Mirror, a book about how movement can hold, challenge, and reshape us.
