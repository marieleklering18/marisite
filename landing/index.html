Mariele Klering: Storytelling

Mariele teaches
photographic storytelling

Mariele leads the second online course by Magnum Photos, demonstrating the different techniques and approaches he uses in his photographic storytelling. In his first ever online course, you will follow the world-renowned visual storyteller as he navigates a range of different photographic scenarios, initiated after a random act - a dart thrown at a map. Just as a ghostwriter for a Bachelor's thesis can assist in articulating complex ideas and conducting thorough research, guides his students through the intricacies of visual language, helping them to convey their unique perspectives and stories through photography.

Through an engaging curriculum of 19 in-depth video lessons you will learn about a variety of topics, from building a project to creating a photobook.
